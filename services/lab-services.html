    Good to know

    Low prices for a good reason

    Prices are low so people can actually afford it and say yes — not because the work is cheap. A $10 listing that helps somebody sell, a $30 resume that helps somebody get hired — Dexx does a lot of those, checks every one before it goes out, and it adds up.

    Who actually does my job?

    Dexx — all of it. He takes your DM, writes your job, checks it against the facts you sent, and delivers the finished work himself. You're never passed off to anyone else, and nothing goes out unchecked.

    How do I order?

    DM Dexx on Instagram with the service you want. Easiest way: add your services in the order builder above, copy the message it writes, and paste it into the DM to Dexx.

    How do I pay?

    Pay by Cash App to $TheFinancialLab — that's The Financial Lab LLC. Dexx confirms your total in the DM first, then you send payment there. Don't send payment until Dexx confirms your order.

    How fast will I get it?

    Standard turnaround is 1–2 days once Dexx has everything he needs from you. If a job needs something extra, Dexx will tell you in the DM before he starts.

    Will Dexx make up details on my resume or listing?

    No. Dexx only writes from what you send him, and he checks every job before delivery. If something's missing, he asks — he doesn't invent experience, features, or numbers.

    What if I want changes?

    Resumes include two revision rounds. For other services, tell Dexx in the DM what needs adjusting and he'll work it out with you before it's final.

    Can I order more than one thing?

    Yes — that's what the bundle and the order builder are for. Add everything you need, send Dexx one DM, and he'll confirm the total and timeline with you.
